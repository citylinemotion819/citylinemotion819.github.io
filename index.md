# 📰 omafeed - Your Simple & Private RSS Reader

Keeping up with your favorite websites and news sources shouldn't be complicated. omafeed brings all your RSS feeds together in one clean, easy-to-use desktop app. No ads, no tracking, just your news.

## ✨ What is omafeed?

omafeed is a free, open-source RSS feed reader designed specifically for the **Omarchy** Linux environment. It lets you subscribe to any website that offers an RSS feed (like blogs, news sites, and YouTube channels) and read all their latest articles in a single, distraction-free window.

Think of it as your personal newspaper, where *you* decide what goes on the front page. It's fast, it's light, and it respects your privacy by keeping your reading habits on your own computer.

## 💡 Why Choose omafeed?

- **🗂️ All-in-One:** Stop jumping between dozens of websites. View all your favorite content from one place.
- **🔒 Private & Secure:** No cloud accounts, no data mining. Your feed subscriptions and reading history stay right on your device.
- **⚡ Fast & Lightweight:** Built for Linux, omafeed is quick to launch and low on system resources, even if you follow hundreds of feeds.
- **🛠️ Customizable:** Organize your feeds into categories, mark items as read or unread, and adjust the layout to suit your reading style.
- **🎨 Clean Interface:** A simple, modern design that focuses on your content, not on clutter.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Tips

If you run into any problems, here are a few quick fixes for common issues:

- **"Download doesn't start":** Sometimes, a browser pop-up blocker can prevent the download. Try clicking the download button again and allow the pop-up.
- **"The application won't run":** On some Linux distributions, you may need to right-click the downloaded file, go to **Properties**, and check the **"Allow executing file as program"** box.
- **"Can't add a feed":** Make sure you are copying the correct RSS feed URL (it usually contains `rss`, `feed`, or `atom` in the link).
- **"The app is slow":** If you're following thousands of feeds, try pausing the refresh for feeds you rarely read, or reduce how often omafeed checks for new articles.

If you're still having trouble, you can check the "Issues" tab on the GitHub repository for solutions or to report a problem. The community is always happy to help.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**What is an RSS feed?**
An RSS feed is a standardized format that lets websites share their latest articles. Subscribing to a feed means you'll get a notification whenever that site posts something new, without having to visit the site itself.

**Is omafeed really free?**
Yes, 100% free. It's an open-source project, meaning its code is public and anyone can contribute or modify it. It has no in-app purchases or hidden costs.

**Can I import my feeds from another reader?**
Yes, omafeed supports the standard OPML file format, which most RSS readers use to export and import feed lists. You can easily transfer your subscriptions.

**What makes omafeed different from browser-based readers?**
omafeed is a native desktop application. It's faster, uses fewer resources, works offline (to view cached articles), and feels more responsive than a web-based service. It also ensures your data stays on your machine.

## 🛡️ Safe and Secure

omafeed is built with your privacy in mind. There is no online account, no telemetry, and no way for us to see what you read. All your subscriptions stored locally on your hard drive. The application only connects to the internet to fetch the RSS feeds you've requested.
