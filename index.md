## 🛠 Features

The widget sits on your desktop to provide constant access to your data. You do not need to open a browser to check your status.

### Usage tracking
The widget displays your current quota. It updates in real time as you work. You see the exact amount of credits used during your session.

### Credit expiry
Credit cycles can be hard to track. This tool shows the date and time your credits reset. You can plan your development work to match your schedule.

### Token trends
Understanding how you use tokens improves your efficiency. The widget keeps a record of token usage over hours, days, and weeks. You can spot patterns in your development workflow.

### Route analytics
The app tracks your local Skill and Agent routes. It lists which tools consume the most requests. This helps you identify where to optimize your configuration.

* Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11.
* Memory: At least 256 MB of available RAM.
* Storage: 50 MB of free disk space.
* Connection: An active internet connection for data synchronization.
* Permissions: You must have permission to install software on your machine.

## ⚙️ Configuration

The app works as soon as you launch it. You might need to provide a key or an authorization token to link your account. 

### Setting your account
1. Right-click the orb widget.
2. Select Settings.
3. Enter your account token in the provided field.
4. Save your changes.

The widget updates its display within seconds. If the data appears off, check your internet connection or verify your token.

## 🛡 Security and privacy

This tool stores information locally on your computer. Your usage patterns and analytics do not leave your device unless you choose to sync them. The local-first approach ensures that your workspace data remains under your control. The widget uses encrypted storage for all keys and configuration files.

## 📝 Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues during installation or usage, try these steps:

* Ensure you run the installer as an administrator.
* Check that your Windows version meets the requirements.
* Close other widget software if you experience screen flickering.
* Restart the application if the data feed stops updating.
* Check your firewall settings if the widget cannot retrieve your quota status.

## ❓ Frequently asked questions

Does this tool record my code? 
No. The tool only tracks metadata related to your quota and route analysis. It does not read your source files.

Can I move the orb? 
Yes. Click and hold the center of the orb to drag it to any location on your screen.

How do I close the application? 
Right-click the orb and select Exit. You can also close it from the taskbar tray menu.

Does the orb stay on top of other windows? 
By default, the orb stays on top. You can change this behavior in the settings menu by toggling the "Always on top" feature.
